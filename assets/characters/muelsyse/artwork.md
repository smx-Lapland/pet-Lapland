# 缪尔赛思示例立绘

`portrait.png` 是为角色迁移示例制作的同人立绘，使用内置 ImageGen 从文字生成，未使用游戏官方立绘作参考图。立绘可在「角色与迁移 → 角色与形象」中替换。

## 生成提示词

Use case: stylized-concept. A single transparent PNG desktop companion sprite, original chibi fan illustration of Muelsyse from Arknights, friendly Rhine Lab water researcher. Full body, long flowing light mint-green hair, distinct long pointed elf ears, amber eyes, white research coat over a dark practical outfit, one small suspended water droplet near her raised hand. Three-quarter front view, warm inquisitive expression, readable silhouette and clean edges at 130 px, understated anime cel shading. Center and tightly frame the complete character with a narrow transparent margin. No text, logos, ground or backdrop.
